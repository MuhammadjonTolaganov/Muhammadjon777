Scope: the GreenHaven storefront (home, shop, product, cart, checkout, track, care, about, contact) — one hash-routed surface. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: retail plant buyers worldwide, five languages at parity, no lead market. Job: choose a plant they can keep alive, then keep it alive. Action: add to cart. Proof: the care sheet and the AI doctor, both already real. Constraint: five scripts (Latin, Cyrillic, Arabic RTL, Simplified Chinese) and five currencies; no backend; all catalogue figures are placeholder and must not be presented as fact.

## Direction contract

THESIS: GreenHaven is an enclosed garden the visitor walks into, organised as a place rather than a catalogue. It refuses the arrangement every competitor ships — cream ground, serif headline beside one hero plant, a row of four equal category cards underneath — and refuses its opposite, the dark jungle with a neon accent.

OWN-WORLD: Chahar bagh, the four-quarter paradise garden. Ground is glazed zellige and lime plaster, not paper: deep teal #0E3B43, turquoise #1C7C8C, terracotta #C1573C, saffron #E3B23C, plaster #F2EADC, ink #10211F. Tessellation is structure — it divides the page — never wallpaper behind a normal layout. Every action sits on an engraved brass plaque. Section breaks are carved screens (mashrabiya), not hairlines. Water is the only still surface. Display per script so no language is a transliteration of another: Marcellus (Latin), Alegreya (Cyrillic), Amiri (Arabic), Noto Serif SC (Chinese); body Commissioner, Noto Sans Arabic, Noto Sans SC.

STORY: the visitor sees a garden seen from above and understands immediately that this is a place with rooms, not a shop with filters. They enter a quarter, find a plant whose care they can meet, and buy it. They leave knowing the garden has a keeper who answers in their own language.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a full-bleed four-quarter garden plan inside a tiled border, filling the screen under the header. The two axes are water channels; the horizontal channel is the live search field, set into the water. Each quarter is a category — Indoor, Outdoor, Succulents, Flowering — carrying its name, its count, and one plant, and the whole quarter is the control that enters it. The primary action sits bottom-left on a brass plaque reading Explore Plants; the AI doctor sits opposite on a smaller plaque. Below the fold the page continues as the garden's sections, never as a second hero.
